# CAP5771-Project

## The Heilmeier Catechism

1. What are you trying to do? Articulate your objectives using absolutely no jargon.
   1. We are trying to find out when bad air quality leads to an increase in respiratory related hospitalizations so that health care agencies can prepare in advance.

2. How is it done today, and what are the limits of current practice?
   1. Air quality data is recorded by environmental agencies, while health and hospitalization data is recorded by healthcare agencies. They are typically analyzed separately. Health responses usually do not happen until after there is an increase in hospitalizations.
